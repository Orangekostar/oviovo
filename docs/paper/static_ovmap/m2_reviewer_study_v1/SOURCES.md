## Claim discipline

Source facts, mathematical consequences, proposed designs, and measured outcomes are different categories. Canonical-score compression, vote degeneracy and softmax vocabulary dependence follow from the formulas; they are not precomputed explanations of every recovered/lost object. No supplement experiment in this pack has already been run. Calibration, probability means, ensemble diversity and geometry/semantics decoupling are not claimed as newly invented techniques.
