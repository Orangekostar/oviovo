## Mathematical prior art (primary sources, verified in this turn)

- [Li et al., Contrastive Decoding, ACL 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.687/): an expert/reference likelihood difference with a plausibility constraint. This supports treating log-probability differences as existing tools, not claiming subtraction as original. The current proposal concerns matched 3D-region evidence and mass-constrained class updates, not text generation or a reproduction of that paper.
- [Jiang et al., Statistical ranking and combinatorial Hodge theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.1067): recovering global rankings from pairwise comparisons and diagnosing inconsistent cycles through least-squares graph structure. The ranking solver itself is prior art; the candidate research contribution would be the evidence construction and useful dependency model.
- [Krause et al., Near-Optimal Sensor Placements in Gaussian Processes, JMLR 2008](https://www.jmlr.org/beta/papers/v9/krause08a.html): information-based observation placement under stated probabilistic assumptions. It does not establish the correctness or approximation guarantees of the proposed covariance proxy or future acquisition policy.

No exhaustive novelty search has established that the combined proposed mechanisms are first-of-kind. No guarantee of AP improvement is inferred from a KL identity, PSD kernel, convex QP or unique graph solution.
