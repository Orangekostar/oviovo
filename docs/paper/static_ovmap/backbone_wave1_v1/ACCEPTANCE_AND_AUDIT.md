# Acceptance, audit and interpretation

## A. Package preparation audit versus actual experiments

This package was prepared by reviewing the uploaded roadmap and the pinned repository files listed in SOURCE_EVIDENCE. The accompanying audit script validates the instruction's internal IDs, counts, scope, constants, links and synthetic kernels. It **does not** execute native C++, SAM2, a server dataset, source temperatures, real Q_GAIN or the official evaluator.

The12 synthetic tests include an explicit300-pixel mutation-order counterexample, ID-renaming invariance, disjoint simultaneous fragments, empty residual, preservation of small carved intersections, directional matching versus its transpose, unmatched handling, native-ratio activation, inclusive SAM frame bounds and percentage-point selection bands. They establish useful arithmetic boundaries, not that the real dataset contains the same failure or that any method improves.

A family technical failure can coexist with completed structural experiments, but not with a claim of full experimental coverage. Negative complete results are useful and must be released.

## C. Manual review corrections included

The review corrected the earlier mode3-only claim; distinguished countowner from graph components; introduced the simple native-ratio control; preserved the native exporter; separated old and new native anchors; bounded temporal processing without future conditioning; froze all readout rules; expanded selection to four already-exposed development scenes; limited transfer/combinations; and prevented cache sharing from distorting efficiency comparisons.

Do not rerun every historical audit to validate this study. Targeted implementation tests and actual scientific comparisons are the relevant work. The final production checklist belongs in BACKBONE_WAVE1_HANDOFF and must link actual receipts, not this package's synthetic-test log.

## D. Unsupported statements even after software runs

Do not claim independent generalization from these already-exposed scenes, statistical significance from preference bands, online full-system real-time performance from offline cached readout, or a novel covariance/geometry theory from an unchanged simple control. Do not claim a stronger backbone solely because semantic availability/ranking changed. State which of BB00/RATIO/FORWARD/RAW each retained mechanism actually surpassed and where it lost.
