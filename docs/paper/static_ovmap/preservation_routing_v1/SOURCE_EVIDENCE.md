## Literature and documentation: what is borrowed, what is not claimed

**L01 — Mask-Adapter (CVPR2025).** [CVF paper page](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Li_Mask-Adapter_The_Devil_is_in_the_Masks_for_Open-Vocabulary_Segmentation_CVPR_2025_paper.html). It learns semantic activation for region classification and studies mask robustness. Its head is an external matched baseline, not our new invention. We retain the numerical source already bound by the parent; we do not claim reproduction of its full published training recipe.

**L02 — Robust Fine-Tuning of Zero-Shot Models / WiSE-FT (CVPR2022).** [CVF paper page](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Wortsman_Robust_Fine-Tuning_of_Zero-Shot_Models_CVPR_2022_paper.html). It documents the tension between adaptation and distribution-shift robustness and studies weight ensembling. It motivates checking preservation; our conditional embedding preservation and function subtraction are **not** presented as WiSE-FT or as a proved remedy.

**L03 — PyTorch autograd mechanics.** [Official documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/autograd). Frozen parameters need `requires_grad=False`; that is different from disabling recording around operations whose inputs require gradients. The task retains the existing environment, not whatever version the latest documentation displays.

## Judgments versus hypotheses

Supported algebra: common shifts cancel in a within-group softmax. A fixed null route permits total local contribution to vanish; plain renormalization among only low-confidence groups does not. A functional residual `f_theta-f_init` is zero when parameters match. These can be checked numerically.

Not established: that softmax cancellation explains all LR accuracy loss; that conditional preservation improves unseen classes; that group gating improves AP; that H2 exists on disk; that generated real proposals match enough objects; or that this complete method is novel relative to all literature. No count in this instruction pack is a new measured accuracy result.

The R/G equations, gate thresholds, training budget, H2 hash prefix and small real-proposal diagnostic are **predeclared new design choices**. Direct paired comparisons and actual transfer results must determine whether they deserve contribution status.
